01

The problem

Traditional wishlists fall short: friends accidentally buy the same gift, expensive gifts are hard to coordinate, reservations often reveal who bought what and ruin the surprise, sharing across messaging apps is cumbersome, and few solutions support multilingual users or modern messaging integrations. Giftbede was built as a privacy-first social experience to fix that.

02

What I built

Giftbede is built around one idea: share one link and let everyone coordinate gifts privately. The platform includes personal wishlists, private and partial gift reservations, smart gift previews, Telegram notifications, private group chats for co-reservers, public profile sharing, social following, and English & Persian (RTL) support, all with privacy, usability, and mobile-first interactions in mind.

  • Wishlist management

    create and organise wishlists, smart gift previews, image uploads, shareable public profiles

  • Private reservations

    anonymous reservations, partial reservations, expiry, reserved gifts dashboard

  • Telegram integration

    Telegram Login, instant notifications, bot connection flow, presence-aware alerts

  • Group collaboration

    private reserver group chats, seen indicators, presence tracking, shared gift coordination

  • Social features

    follow creators, public follower lists, profile sharing, activity updates

  • Multilingual experience

    English, Persian (RTL), automatic layout switching, localised interface
